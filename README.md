## Hello 👋

🌐 Website: [mcanzi.de](https://mcanzi.de)

I am a Data Scientist and LingLab Manager at the Department of Linguistics of the University of Konstanz. I work on multimodal speech perception, acoustic phonetics and physiological methods, and I teach statistics in R.

### 📦 R packages

- 👓 [glasses3](https://githu.com/mcanzi/glasses3): reads recordings from the Tobii Pro Glasses 3 wearable eye tracker into R ([documentation](https://mcanzi.de/glasses3/)).
- 🦍 [silverback](github.com/mcanzi): turns messy Gorilla Experiment Builder downloads into tidy, analysis-ready tibbles of trials, questionnaires and participants, with data checks and anonymised export ([documentation](https://mcanzi.de/silverback)).
- 🎲 [simling](https://github.com/mcanzi/simling): simulated linguistic data for teaching ([documentation](https://mcanzi.de/simling/)).
